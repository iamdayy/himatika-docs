4. Mobile off-centre @390px: 28px left margin vs 10px right. (R-03)

9. Generic CTA "Mulai Jelajahi" + "Mulai jelajahi sekarang!" in hero copy. (R-15)

LOW (Quality Locks):

11. Glass: 2 backdrop-blur surfaces (header + 1). Dose cap 1. (R-10)

14. Paragraph measure ~156 chars on homepage, ~95 on intro (cap 45–75). 
15. Eyebrow "Memulai" above H1 on intro. (house: no eyebrows)
16. Motion: 8 elements moved on homepage (floor 8, warn <16); no moving picture, no footage.
